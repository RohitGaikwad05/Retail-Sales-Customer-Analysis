## 📌 Project Overview

Retail businesses generate large amounts of transactional data, but raw data alone cannot explain which products perform best, which customers contribute the most revenue, or where profitability can be improved.

This project transforms retail transaction data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL-based analysis, KPI development, and interactive Power BI dashboards.

**Project focus:** Sales performance • Customer analytics • Product profitability • Business intelligence

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyse overall sales, revenue, and profitability.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour and spending patterns.
- Evaluate regional sales performance.
- Track monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Build an interactive dashboard to support business decision-making.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection** — Load the retail sales dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning** — Inspect missing values, duplicates, data types, and unnecessary columns.
3. **Data Transformation** — Prepare analysis-ready data and derive features such as year, month, quarter, order value, and profit margin.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis** — Examine sales, profit, product categories, regions, and customer behaviour.
5. **SQL Analysis** — Write analytical queries using aggregations, filtering, grouping, CTEs, and window functions.
6. **KPI Development** — Calculate revenue, profit, orders, customers, average order value, and profit margin.
7. **Power BI Development** — Build an interactive, three-page dashboard.
8. **Business Insights** — Interpret findings and identify opportunities for improvement.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard is organised into three analytical pages.

### 1. Executive Overview

- Total revenue, total profit, orders, customers, average order value, and profit margin.
- Monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Sales by product category and region.
- Category-level performance comparisons.

### 2. Customer Insights

- Customer revenue and average spending.
- Top customers by revenue.
- Customer segment analysis.
- Customer ranking and repeat-purchase analysis.

### 3. Product Performance

- Top-performing products by sales.
- Product-level revenue and profitability.
- Category revenue and profit comparisons.
- Product ranking and margin analysis.

> **View the dashboard:** Open the Power BI file in the `powerbi/` folder using Power BI Desktop. Dashboard screenshots are available in the `screenshots/` folder.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- The dataset records approximately **$12.64 million in revenue** and **$1.47 million in profit**.
- The overall profit margin is approximately **11.61%**.
- Technology is a leading category for revenue and profit in the analysed data.
- Profitability varies across product categories, highlighting the importance of analysing margins alongside sales.
- Regional and customer-level analysis helps identify key contributors to business performance.
- Product-level rankings provide a basis for investigating high-performing and underperforming items.

*These findings describe the analysed dataset and should not be interpreted as universal retail market trends.*
